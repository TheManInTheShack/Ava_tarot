# Seven of Pentacles

A figure leans on their hoe, resting, gazing at the seven pentacles growing on the vine before them. They have worked; the vine is producing. Now they pause, not from laziness but from wisdom — taking stock, measuring what has grown, deciding whether the investment is yielding the right return.

## Upright

The midpoint of a long endeavor. Things are growing, but slowly and through sustained effort. The Seven of Pentacles is not a flashy card — it speaks to the patient work of building: a career, a garden, a creative practice, a financial plan. The pause it depicts is not delay but discernment. This is the moment to assess: is what I'm growing worth tending? Is the return worth the labor?

## Reversed

The assessment turns unfavorable. The work put in is not matching the results coming out, and it's time to reconsider the investment. Can also indicate impatience — abandoning something just before it would have yielded, or expecting faster results than the thing can produce.
