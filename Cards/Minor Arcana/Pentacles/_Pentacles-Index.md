# Suit of Pentacles

**Element**: Earth  
**Domain**: Body, material world, money, work, nature, physical health, long-term investment  
**Season**: Winter into Spring  
**Direction**: North  
**Court personalities**: Practical, reliable, patient, hardworking, grounded, occasionally stubborn or overly cautious

The Pentacles suit traces the arc of material experience: from the seed of abundance (Ace) through effort, hardship, generosity, patience, and finally the King's stewardship of resources. Pentacles are the suit of the physical world — what is built, what is earned, what sustains the body and the community.
