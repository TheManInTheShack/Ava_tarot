# Queen of Pentacles

A queen sits on her throne, a pentacle in her hands, surrounded by comfort and abundance. She is nurturing and grounded; she has created security and comfort for those around her through practical care and generous abundance.

## Upright

The Queen of Pentacles is nurturing, practical, and abundantly generous. This Queen has created security and comfort through her own effort, and she extends that abundance to others. She is the gardener, the homemaker, the businesswoman who balances profit with generosity. In readings, the Queen often represents a person (or the querent's own inner Queen) who is nurturing, practical, and capable. She brings good news regarding home, family, finances, and the ability to create comfort and security. The Queen celebrates both giving and receiving, both having and sharing.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Queen's nurturing becomes withholding or smothering. Stinginess appears; generosity is denied. Or the Queen is overextended, trying to care for everyone and depleting herself in the process. Sometimes reversed indicates that material comfort is being sought to the exclusion of emotional nurturing.
