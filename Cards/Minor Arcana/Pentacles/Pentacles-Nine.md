# Nine of Pentacles

A figure stands in a garden of abundance, surrounded by pentacles. They are well-dressed, at ease, clearly comfortable and secure. A bird perches nearby; the garden flourishes. This is the card of earned abundance and the satisfaction of having created wealth and security through effort.

## Upright

The Nine of Pentacles is abundance, wealth, and the independence that comes from having successfully accumulated security. This card appears when financial stability has been achieved, when the results of effort are visible, when you can finally rest in the knowledge that you have created something lasting. The Nine celebrates not inherited wealth but earned security—the satisfaction of knowing you built this yourself. It often indicates luxury, leisure well-deserved, and the comfort of self-sufficiency.

## Reversed

Reversed, the abundance is lost, threatened, or never secured. Lack appears; financial difficulty or dependence. Or the abundance exists but brings no satisfaction because it was not earned through your own effort. Sometimes reversed indicates that wealth has become obsessive—you have plenty but can never feel satisfied.
