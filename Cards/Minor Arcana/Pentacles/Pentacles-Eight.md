# Eight of Pentacles

A figure works at a craft, shaping a pentacle with focus and dedication. Eight pentacles are arranged around them—each one represents practice, development, mastery slowly earned. This is the card of patient skill-building and the devotion that leads to expertise.

## Upright

The Eight of Pentacles is mastery being built through diligent practice and skill-building. This card appears when you are developing expertise, learning a craft, or deepening your capabilities through dedicated effort. The Eight celebrates the apprentice and the master alike—both are on the path of skill. There is no shortcut to mastery, but there is joy in the learning. The Eight of Pentacles indicates that your focused effort will pay off; keep practicing, keep learning, keep refining.

## Reversed

Reversed, the dedication is lacking. Shortcuts are being taken; corners are cut. The craftsmanship suffers because the focus is not sustained. Or potential is being wasted; you are not developing the skills you could. Sometimes reversed indicates that you are practicing the wrong thing or that your effort is misdirected.
