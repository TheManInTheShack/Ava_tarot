# Four of Pentacles

A figure sits surrounded by pentacles, holding them closely, their posture defensive and protective. The figure appears to be guarding what they have, unwilling to let it move. This is the card of holding on, of security achieved through control and resistance to change.

## Upright

The Four of Pentacles is stability through careful control. You have secured something precious—money, status, a position—and you are holding it tightly. This can be prudent conservation or pathological hoarding. The Four asks: are you protecting what you have, or are you imprisoned by the need to hold on? Security is good; refusing to share, invest, or risk anything is stagnation. The Four often indicates that stability has been achieved but at the cost of flexibility.

## Reversed

Reversed, the grip loosens. You release what you were holding; generosity becomes possible. Or control is taken from you; circumstances force change. Sometimes reversed indicates that you are finally willing to invest, share, or risk what you have been protecting. Letting go allows for growth.
