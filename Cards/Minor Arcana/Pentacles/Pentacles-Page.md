# Page of Pentacles

A young figure stands holding a pentacle, gazing at it with wonder and focus. The figure is grounded, practical, yet alive with possibility. This is the archetype of the grounded dreamer, the ambitious youth, the student eager to learn.

## Upright

The Page of Pentacles is potential, ambition, and the willingness to learn. This Page is earnest and focused on practical matters—how to build, how to grow, how to create security. The Page often represents a young person or a message about opportunities, finances, or learning opportunities. Or it may represent your own inner Page: the part that wants to build something real, that takes responsibility seriously, that sees potential in practical effort. Good news regarding opportunities, financial matters, or education.

## Reversed

Reversed, the focus is lost or misdirected. The Page is distracted, careless, or lacks the ambition to pursue opportunity. Or the opportunity does not materialize; the potential is unrealized. Sometimes reversed indicates immaturity or the refusal to take responsibility for your own growth and security.
