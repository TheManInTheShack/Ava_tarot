# Three of Pentacles

Figures work together on a structure, each contributing their skill. Tools are visible; the craftsmanship is evident. This is the card of skilled collaboration, where individual talents combine toward a shared goal.

## Upright

The Three of Pentacles is collaboration and teamwork, where each person's skills are valued and combined toward excellence. This card appears when a project is benefiting from shared effort, when learning is happening through working alongside others, when craftsmanship is being mastered through practice and cooperation. The Three celebrates the quality that comes from respectful collaboration. It often indicates apprenticeship, team projects, or situations where individual skills are being honed through shared work.

## Reversed

Reversed, the collaboration breaks down. Lack of communication, poor teamwork, or individuals whose skills are not complementary. The work suffers; quality is compromised. Or one person is dominating the effort while others are diminished. Sometimes reversed indicates that you are working alone when collaboration would improve the outcome.
