# King of Pentacles

A king sits on his throne, solid and commanding, his crown of grains upon his head, pentacles visible around him. He has built an empire of abundance and security. His power is material, real, and tangible. This is the archetype of the prosperous ruler, the successful businessperson, the one who has transformed potential into concrete wealth.

## Upright

The King of Pentacles is abundance, prosperity, and the power that comes from material success. This King has built something lasting and substantial. He rules through competence and the creation of wealth that benefits his realm. In readings, the King often represents a person (or the querent's own inner King) who is prosperous, powerful, and capable of creating and maintaining abundance. The King brings good news regarding finances, business, material security, and success. His legacy is not just wealth but the systems that create and maintain it.

## Reversed

Reversed, the King's wealth becomes a liability. Greed appears; generosity is absent. Or financial loss occurs; the abundant foundation crumbles. Sometimes reversed indicates that material power has corrupted the King or that wealth is being misused. The reversal asks: what are you doing with what you have created?
