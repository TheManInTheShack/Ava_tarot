# Six of Pentacles

A figure holds a set of scales, balanced, while dispensing coins to those in need. This is fair dealing, generosity that is neither patronizing nor one-sided. The giving flows; the scales remain even.

## Upright

The Six of Pentacles is generosity, fair dealing, and the wisdom of circulation. What you have is meant to be shared; abundance grows through giving. This card appears when you are in a position to help others, when fair exchange is happening, when generosity is appropriate and welcomed. The Six is not careless giving but measured, fair distribution—what each needs. The card celebrates both those who give and those who receive, as both are necessary for balance.

## Reversed

Reversed, the balance is broken. You give too much or too little; the exchange is unfair. Selfishness appears; generosity is withheld. Or you are receiving help that comes with strings attached, creating indebtedness. The scales are tipped; fairness is gone.
