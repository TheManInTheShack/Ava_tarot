# Ten of Pentacles

An elder sits beneath an arch hung with pentacles while a couple and child stand nearby, dogs at their feet. The scene is multigenerational — prosperity built not just for oneself but passed down. The Ten of Pentacles is the completion of the Pentacles journey: from single seed to deep-rooted legacy.

## Upright

Lasting material security, family wealth, and the satisfaction of a life well built. This card often appears when financial security extends beyond the individual — to family, community, or future generations. It honors the work of building something that endures. Also indicates a favorable time for real estate, inheritance, or family business decisions.

## Reversed

The dark side of legacy: financial failure, family conflict over inheritance, or the burden of maintaining a structure that no longer serves. Sometimes reversed Ten of Pentacles indicates that what was built has become a cage, or that prosperity exists on paper but not in lived experience.

## Elemental and Astrological

Earth at its most mature and established — not the fresh potential of the Ace but the deep-rooted oak. The Pentacles suit completes its arc here: from seed to harvest to legacy.
