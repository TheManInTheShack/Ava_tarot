# Five of Pentacles

Two figures trudge through snow past a lit stained-glass window — one on crutches, one in thin clothing. The warmth and shelter of the church are just behind the glass, yet they do not enter. Whether they don't know it's there, feel unworthy to enter, or have simply not looked up is left open. The Five of Pentacles is about material hardship, but it is equally about the psychology of deprivation.

## Upright

Financial difficulty, material insecurity, or the feeling of being left out in the cold. This card can speak to literal poverty or job loss, but it also addresses the inner experience of lack: the belief that help is unavailable, that one is beyond caring for, that the warmth is always behind glass. The window is lit — the card asks whether the figures have noticed.

## Reversed

The door opens, or the turn arrives. Financial improvement, or the decision to accept help that was there all along. Reversed Five of Pentacles often marks the moment when someone stops insisting on suffering alone and allows community, support, or resources to reach them.
