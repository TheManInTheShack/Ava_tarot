# Knight of Pentacles

A figure on horseback moves at a steady, deliberate pace, a pentacle held carefully before them. This is not the rapid charge of Fire or Air but the steady march of determination. The Knight's bearing is responsible and trustworthy; the movement is sure and careful.

## Upright

The Knight of Pentacles is diligence, reliability, and the steady progress that comes from devoted effort. This Knight does not dash; they move methodically, attending to details, ensuring quality. In readings, the Knight often represents a person (or the querent's own inner Knight) who is responsible, hardworking, and trustworthy. This Knight brings good news regarding practical matters, work, financial security, and steady progress toward goals. The Knight's movement is slow but sure; what is built will last.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Knight's diligence becomes complacency or laziness. Progress stalls; the steady march becomes immobile. Or the Knight is unreliable, not following through on commitments. Sometimes reversed indicates that Earth-bound practicality is preventing you from taking necessary risks or dreaming bigger.
