# Two of Wands

A figure stands on a castle parapet, holding a globe and gazing out at the sea and distant lands. One wand is fixed to the wall behind him; the other he holds. The Ace sparked the fire — now the Two asks what you will do with it. The world is wide; the question is which horizon to sail toward.

## Upright

Planning, vision, and the courage to step beyond the familiar. You have the spark; now chart the course. The Two of Wands favors bold decisions made from a broad perspective — look past the immediate and consider the larger picture. It often appears before a significant journey, project, or change of direction.

## Reversed

Hesitation at the threshold. The plan exists but fear or lack of confidence keeps it theoretical. Sometimes reversed indicates poor planning, or a tendency to hold the globe without ever setting sail. Ask what is actually holding you back — practical obstacle, or the fear of leaving the known behind?

## Elemental and Astrological

Pure Wands energy moving from potential into planning. The fire of the Ace becomes directional here — it wants to travel, expand, discover.
