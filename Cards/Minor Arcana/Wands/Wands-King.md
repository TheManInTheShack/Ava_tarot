# King of Wands

The King sits on a throne decorated with lions and salamanders — symbols of fire and transformation — holding a flowering wand. He leans forward slightly, as if about to spring into action. Behind him, a salamander (fire lizard) crawls, echoing those on his robe. He is fire fully mature: the visionary who doesn't just dream but builds, who doesn't just lead but inspires.

## Upright

Mastery of fire energy in its most complete expression. The King of Wands is the entrepreneur, the creative director, the person who makes bold decisions and carries others along by the force of their conviction. This is leadership through vision and genuine passion, not through authority alone. When this card appears as a person, they are someone worth following — and when it appears as advice, it is calling the querent to step into that role.

## Reversed

Fire without wisdom. The King's forcefulness tips into domination; his confidence becomes arrogance; his bold action becomes recklessness. Others are steamrolled rather than inspired. Reversed King of Wands can also indicate someone who has the vision but is too scattered or impatient to execute it.
