# Seven of Wands

A figure stands on a raised position, wielding a wand against six others rising from below. They appear to be wearing mismatched shoes — one on, one off — suggesting they were caught off-guard. Yet they hold their ground. The high position is an advantage, but it is also the reason others are climbing up.

## Upright

The price of success is opposition. The Seven of Wands appears when someone who has achieved something — a position, a reputation, an idea they've championed — now faces pushback, competition, or challenge. The card calls for standing firm. This is not paranoia; the challengers are real. But the high ground is held by those who refuse to be pushed off it.

## Reversed

Capitulation under pressure that didn't actually require it. The figure steps off the raised position — out of exhaustion, self-doubt, or simply not believing the thing is worth defending. Sometimes reversed Seven of Wands indicates genuine overwhelm, where the challenges have become truly unsustainable and strategic retreat is wiser than continued defense.
