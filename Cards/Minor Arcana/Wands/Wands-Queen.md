# Queen of Wands

A queen sits on her throne, holding a wand in one hand and a sunflower in the other. Her eyes are bright with intelligence and fire; her bearing is regal and assured. At her feet, a black lion rests, tamed by her presence. This is fire mastered—passion directed toward creation and leadership.

## Upright

The Queen of Wands is confident, creative, and magnetic. She knows what she wants and pursues it with charm and determination. The Queen embodies the full expression of Fire: passion, creativity, leadership, and the charisma that draws others to her. She is independent, not seeking permission, and she lights up those around her. In readings, the Queen often represents a person (or the querent's own inner Queen) who is confident in their power, creative in their expression, and unafraid to shine. She brings good news, especially regarding creative projects, business ventures, and bold action.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Queen's fire becomes destructive. Domination rather than leadership; jealousy rather than confidence; aggression rather than assertion. The creative block appears; the Queen cannot access her own power. Or the confidence is false, masking deep insecurity. Sometimes reversed Queen indicates a person who uses charm to manipulate or who has temporarily lost connection to their own strength.
