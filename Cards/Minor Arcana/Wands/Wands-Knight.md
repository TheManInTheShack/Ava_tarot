# Knight of Wands

A figure on horseback races forward with wand held high, the horse itself seeming to leap with energy and passion. Fire streams around both rider and steed. This is action incarnate, passion unleashed, the moment of commitment to forward movement.

## Upright

The Knight of Wands is fire in motion—passionate, adventurous, action-oriented. This is the energy of someone who cannot sit still, who leaps at opportunity, who is driven by inspiration and enthusiasm. The Knight often indicates travel, relocation, swift change, or a person who embodies this restless, passionate energy. In readings, the Knight suggests movement is happening or needed, that passion should be channeled into action. The card celebrates drive and courage but also warns: a Knight without direction is reckless. The energy must be aimed at something.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Knight's energy becomes scattered or blocked. Impatience leads to mistakes; haste leads to regret. Or the Knight's journey is delayed—the travel plans fall through, the adventure is postponed. Sometimes reversed Knight indicates absence—someone important is away or unavailable. Or recklessness: passion without wisdom, action without thought.
