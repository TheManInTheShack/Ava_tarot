# Suit of Wands

**Element**: Fire  
**Domain**: Will, creativity, passion, career, ambition, spirituality  
**Season**: Summer  
**Direction**: South  
**Court personalities**: Adventurous, enthusiastic, entrepreneurial, passionate, occasionally impulsive

## The Wands Journey

The Wands suit traces the arc of a creative or professional endeavor from initial spark to mature mastery:

| # | Card | Stage |
|---|------|-------|
| 1 | **Ace** | The creative spark; raw Fire potential |
| 2 | **Two** | Standing on the threshold, looking out; the bold first decision |
| 3 | **Three** | First fruits of effort; ships on the horizon; expanding vision |
| 4 | **Four** | Celebration; harvest; a moment of stability and joy |
| 5 | **Five** | Competition, conflict, chaos; the messy middle |
| 6 | **Six** | Victory; recognition; returning home in triumph |
| 7 | **Seven** | Holding your ground; defending a hard-won position |
| 8 | **Eight** | Rapid movement; messages in flight; acceleration |
| 9 | **Nine** | Near the end, battered but still standing; resilience |
| 10 | **Ten** | Overburdened; the weight of success; need to delegate or release |
| 11 | **Page** | The eager beginner; the message of new inspiration |
| 12 | **Knight** | The fast-moving adventurer; passion fully in action |
| 13 | **Queen** | Charismatic, confident, magnetic; fire with warmth and steadiness |
| 14 | **King** | Visionary leader; entrepreneur; Fire fully mastered |
