# Three of Wands

A figure stands on a cliff edge, staff in hand, watching ships sail out on the water below. Two more wands are planted beside them. They have already acted — the ships are launched, the plan is in motion — and now they watch the horizon for what returns. The Three of Wands is not waiting anxiously; it is watching with confidence.

## Upright

The Ace planted the spark, the Two made the decision, and now the Three watches the results expand outward. This is the card of the entrepreneur who has sent something into the world and is now positioned for what comes back. Long-range vision, expansion beyond the immediate, confidence in a plan already set in motion. Often indicates overseas connections, travel for work, or projects with broader reach than initially imagined.

## Reversed

The ships are delayed, or haven't returned as expected. Plans that seemed solid encounter friction — external obstacles, missed connections, poor timing. Sometimes reversed Three of Wands indicates a plan that was launched without adequate foresight, now showing its cracks.
