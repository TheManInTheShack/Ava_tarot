# Four of Wands

Four wands are bound together by garlands of flowers and fruit. Below, figures dance in celebration, a home or castle visible in the distance. This is the first moment of stability and rest after the Three's active expansion. The Four of Wands is the harvest celebrated, the effort rewarded, the community gathered to acknowledge what has been built.

## Upright

The Four of Wands appears when effort bears fruit and celebration is earned. A milestone has been reached; the project is solid; the foundation holds. This is community and belonging—a sense that what you have built resonates with others, that you are not alone in your efforts. Home, harmony, a place of safety and rest. The card often indicates actual celebrations: weddings, promotions, family gatherings, holidays. There is stability now after the forward momentum of the Three. Pause here to acknowledge what has been accomplished.

## Reversed

Reversed, the celebration is delayed, marred, or missing. Conflict disrupts what should be harmony. The foundation is unstable—what seemed solid proves fragile. Or you are celebrating alone, isolated from community, unable to share your success. The invitation to the feast did not arrive. Reversed suggests the need to address instability before returning to festivity.
