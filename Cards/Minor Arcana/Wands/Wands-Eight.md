# Eight of Wands

Eight wands fly through the air, their trajectory swift and true, their flight leaving a trail across the sky. Below, a landscape blurs with speed. This is motion itself—purposeful, directed, rapid movement. The Eight of Wands is the card of momentum and swift progress.

## Upright

The Eight of Wands appears when things move fast. Momentum builds; progress accelerates; communication flows. Projects move forward quickly; decisions are made; the path clears. This is a card of exciting velocity—things are finally happening after a period of slow movement. Messages arrive; opportunities present themselves rapidly; you are moving toward your goal with focused speed. Expect quick changes and be ready to move with them. Travel, swift communications, rapid development all fall under this card.

## Reversed

Reversed, the momentum is blocked or delayed. What should move quickly stalls; the wands fall from the sky. Communication is garbled; miscunderstandings arise. Things move in a different direction than intended, or not at all. The frustration of being stuck after expecting motion. Sometimes reversed Eight indicates that speed is not appropriate—rushing leads to mistakes; what needs careful attention is being rushed through.
