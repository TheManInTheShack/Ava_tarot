# Page of Wands

A young figure holds a wand aloft, gazing at it with fascination and wonder, as if seeing possibility pour from the wood itself. The landscape behind is open and full of promise. This is the archetype of youthful enthusiasm, the explorer, the one who says yes to adventure.

## Upright

The Page of Wands is the embodiment of enthusiasm, curiosity, and the willingness to explore. This is the energy of a new beginning, raw potential, the fire that has not yet been channeled into form. The Page brings good news, excitement, the sense that something new wants to begin. In readings, the Page often represents a messenger, new information, or a young person in the querent's life. Or it may represent the querent's own inner child—that part that wants to play, explore, and discover without agenda. The Page asks: what are you curious about? What calls to you?

## Reversed

Reversed, the enthusiasm becomes scattered or reckless. The Page charges forward without considering consequences, starts projects without finishing them, follows impulses that lead nowhere. Or the Page is blocked—the curiosity is suppressed, the enthusiasm denied, the potential unrealized. Sometimes reversed indicates impatience or the need to slow down and think before acting.
