# Ten of Wands

A figure bent nearly double carries a bundle of ten wands, trudging toward a distant town. They can barely see ahead — the load blocks their view. They will make it; the town is visible. But the cost of this load is evident in their posture. The fire that began as a single Ace spark has accumulated into everything: all the responsibility, all the work.

## Upright

You have taken on more than is comfortable, and you are carrying it anyway. The Ten of Wands acknowledges the weight without asking you to put it down — yet. Completion is near; the load has meaning. This card often appears for people who have built something significant and now bear all the obligations that come with it. The question it raises: is all of this yours to carry, or have you taken on burdens that belong to others?

## Reversed

The burden has become unsustainable. It's time to delegate, to release, to admit that carrying everything alone was never the plan. Reversed Ten of Wands can indicate burnout already in progress, or the moment of deciding to set something down before it breaks you.
