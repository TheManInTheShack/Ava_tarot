# Six of Wands

A figure on horseback carries a wand crowned with laurels, while a crowd below acknowledges the victory. The rider sits straight, assured and glowing. This is the moment of recognition, when the struggle has been won and the community celebrates the victor.

## Upright

The Six of Wands is victory, achievement, and recognition. You have competed and won; your effort is acknowledged. This card brings confidence and the authority that comes from demonstrated success. Good news arrives; accolades are offered; doors open because of your reputation and capability. The Six is more social than the Four's private celebration—the victory is public, witnessed, validated by others. Beware the temptation to rest on laurels, however; the Six's confidence can become arrogance if not grounded in ongoing effort.

## Reversed

Reversed, the victory is denied, diminished, or illusory. You expected recognition and did not receive it. The triumph was smaller than imagined. Or the confidence was false, built on exaggerated sense of your own abilities. Arrogance precedes a fall; the Six reversed warns that what goes up may come down. Sometimes reversed simply means your success is private, not publicly acknowledged, which is not failure but a different path.
