# Eight of Cups

A figure walks away from eight cups arranged on the ground, moving toward the mountains and an uncertain future. The cups are full but abandoned. The figure carries only a staff, facing the unknown. This is the card of necessary departure, the choice to leave what no longer serves even if it was once valuable.

## Upright

The Eight of Cups appears when you must leave something behind—a situation, relationship, or circumstance that no longer nurtures your growth. This is not flight from danger but conscious departure toward something better. The cups represent what you are leaving: comfort, known quantities, emotional security. But they are not enough; your soul calls you to move forward. The Eight of Cups asks courage for the leaving and faith in the unknown ahead. Often indicates necessary travel, relocation, or the end of a chapter in order to begin a new one.

## Reversed

Reversed, the figure cannot move. Fear of the unknown, resistance to necessary change, attachment to what is familiar even when it no longer serves. Or the wandering has become endless—you are running from something rather than moving toward something. The Eight reversed asks: what are you refusing to leave? What are you running from rather than toward?
