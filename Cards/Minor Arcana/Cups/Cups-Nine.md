# Nine of Cups

A figure sits with arms spread wide, a smile of deep contentment on their face, surrounded by nine cups arranged in an arc behind them. This is the wish card, the card of desire fulfilled and satisfaction achieved. The figure is not triumphant but genuinely happy, at peace.

## Upright

The Nine of Cups is one of the most fortunate cards in the deck. It indicates that your wishes are coming true, that satisfaction and emotional fulfillment are present or arriving. This is not the public success of the Wands' Six but internal, emotional completion. You have what you need; you are content. The Nine of Cups is also sometimes called the "wish card"—if you wish for something during a reading, this card suggests the wish will be granted. The card celebrates genuine happiness without needing external validation.

## Reversed

Reversed, the satisfaction is illusory or incomplete. What you thought would make you happy has not, or is being withheld. Overindulgence that masks underlying dissatisfaction; surface happiness covering deeper discontent. Or the card warns of becoming complacent, of resting when more work is needed. The satisfaction is premature or false.
