# King of Cups

A king sits upon his throne, holding a cup and a trident. Behind him, the sea is calm and controlled. His bearing is serene but commanding; he has mastered his emotions without suppressing them. This is the archetype of emotional wisdom and balanced power.

## Upright

The King of Cups is compassion, emotional balance, and the mastery of feelings. This King feels deeply but is not governed by emotion. Instead, emotion is available to them as wisdom and insight. The King of Cups is often a counselor or healer—someone who can hold space for others' pain without being overwhelmed by it. In readings, the King often represents a person (or the querent's own inner King) who has integrated emotional sensitivity with strength and discernment. The King brings good news regarding emotional matters: healing, forgiveness, compassion offered or received.

## Reversed

Reversed, the emotional mastery becomes false control. Emotional manipulation—using sensitivity as a weapon to control others. Or the container breaks: emotional overwhelm, codependency, lack of boundaries. The King inverted cannot balance emotion with action; either emotion rules or it is repressed entirely. Insecurity masked as control.
