# Two of Cups

Two figures face each other, each holding a cup, exchanging them in a gesture of mutual offering. Above them floats the caduceus of Hermes surmounted by a winged lion's head — a symbol of harmony between opposites, of the sacred contract of genuine meeting. This is not infatuation but recognition: I see you, you see me, and what passes between us is real.

## Upright

The card of genuine connection. Whether romantic partnership, deep friendship, creative collaboration, or any relationship grounded in mutual respect and authentic feeling — the Two of Cups marks the moment of true meeting. Both parties are present, both are offering, both are receiving. This card is often considered the minor arcana's most direct love card, but its depth is broader: any bond built on honest recognition.

## Reversed

The exchange has gone asymmetric. One person is more invested, or the recognition that seemed mutual has revealed itself as misreading. Can also indicate a communication breakdown in an otherwise strong bond, or tensions surfacing that have been avoided.
