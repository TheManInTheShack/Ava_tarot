# Ace of Cups

A hand extends from a cloud, offering a cup overflowing with five streams of water — spirit, mind, heart, soul, and the senses. A dove descends bearing a communion wafer. The Ace of Cups is the purest expression of Water: an offering of love, compassion, and emotional possibility that cannot be earned or forced, only received.

## Upright

A new emotional beginning — an opening of the heart. This may manifest as new love, a deepening of compassion, a creative breakthrough, or a spiritual awakening. The Ace of Cups is an invitation: the cup is full and extended to you. Will you drink?

## Reversed

Emotional blockage, repression, or loss. The flow that should move outward is dammed. Reversed Ace of Cups can indicate grief that hasn't been expressed, creativity that's been suppressed, or an inability to open to love. The cup is still there — it has simply been turned away from.

## Elemental and Astrological

Water in its purest elemental form — not yet shaped by suit or situation, just the raw potential of feeling, intuition, and connection.
