# Knight of Cups

A figure on horseback travels slowly, carrying a cup as if it holds something precious. The horse is graceful and controlled; the rider's face is gentle and dreamy. This is the lover, the romantic, the one who offers the heart.

## Upright

The Knight of Cups is romantic, charming, creative, and emotionally expressive. This Knight brings proposals, invitations, messages of love or creative opportunity. In readings, the Knight often represents a person in the querent's life—often a young man, but not always—who is emotionally sensitive, artistic, and potentially romantic. Or it may represent your own inner Knight: the part that offers love, creates beauty, and feels deeply. The Knight of Cups is less impulsive than the Wands Knight; this is a slower, more deliberate approach to emotional expression.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Knight's charm becomes manipulation; sincerity becomes false pretense. Moodiness, unreliability, a person whose emotions are unpredictable or who makes promises they do not keep. Or the proposal does not materialize; the invitation is withdrawn. Sometimes reversed indicates that emotional expression is blocked or that you are defending against the vulnerability the Knight requires.
