# Suit of Cups

**Element**: Water  
**Domain**: Emotion, relationships, intuition, dreams, the unconscious, creativity through feeling  
**Season**: Autumn  
**Direction**: West  
**Court personalities**: Empathetic, imaginative, emotionally attuned, relational, occasionally withdrawn

The Cups suit traces the arc of emotional experience: from the open vessel of pure feeling (Ace) through relationship, loss, nostalgia, fantasy, satisfaction, and finally the full emotional life of the mature King. Where Wands ask "what do I create?", Cups ask "how do I feel, and who do I love?"
