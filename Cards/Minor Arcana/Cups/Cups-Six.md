# Six of Cups

A child and adult exchange cups, surrounded by flowers and a sense of innocent joy. The figure giving appears to be from the past, while the receiver looks toward the future. This is the card of innocence recovered, of the healing power of memory and gentle reconnection.

## Upright

The Six of Cups appears when innocence or happiness returns, often through memory or reunion. This can be literal—reconnection with someone from your past, a place you loved, a version of yourself you had forgotten. The card celebrates the simple, uncomplicated joy of giving and receiving, of generosity without agenda. The Six of Cups often indicates an older person who is helpful or a child in the querent's life. It can also represent inner child recovery—the return to simplicity, play, and trust after a period of hardship. Healing happens through gentleness.

## Reversed

Reversed, the past becomes a trap. Living in nostalgia rather than moving forward; refusing to accept that things have changed. The reunion does not happen or is disappointing. Or naivety becomes a liability—innocence is being exploited or you are ignoring important warnings. Sometimes reversed Six indicates resentment about the way things have changed or refusal to forgive.
