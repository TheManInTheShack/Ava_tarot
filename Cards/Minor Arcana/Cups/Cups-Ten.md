# Ten of Cups

A couple stands with arms raised beneath a rainbow arc of ten cups. Children play nearby; a house sits in the green distance. The scene is domestic, joyful, complete. The Ten of Cups is the emotional summit: not the dramatic peak of new love but the deep satisfaction of a life in which love has been tended and has grown into something that shelters others.

## Upright

Lasting emotional fulfillment. Where the Two of Cups marks the meeting and the beginning, the Ten marks the long fruition of love well tended. Family harmony, deep friendship, a community that genuinely nourishes — this card speaks to the kind of happiness that is not excitement but wholeness. Often appears as confirmation that the querent's emotional life is, or can be, genuinely good.

## Reversed

The picture-perfect surface concealing something hollow underneath. Relationships maintained for appearances, family structures in conflict despite outward harmony, or an emotional life that looks complete but feels empty. Sometimes reversed Ten of Cups simply indicates disruption of a happy home — temporary turbulence rather than fundamental fracture.
