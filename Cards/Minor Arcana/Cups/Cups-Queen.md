# Queen of Cups

The Queen sits on a throne at the water's edge, holding an ornate covered cup — the only one in the deck whose cup is closed, a vessel for what cannot be fully seen. Her feet trail in the water; the sea behind her is calm. She is not above the emotional world but immersed in it, yet she does not drown. Her gift is the capacity to feel deeply without being swept away.

## Upright

Mastery of the emotional and intuitive realm. The Queen of Cups knows what she knows through feeling rather than logic, and she trusts it. She is the deeply empathetic friend, the therapist who genuinely cares, the parent who understands without needing to be told. When this card appears as advice, it asks the querent to lead with compassion and trust their intuitive read of a situation.

## Reversed

The gifts of Water turned against itself. Deep empathy becomes codependency; nurturing becomes martyrdom; intuition becomes anxiety. The Queen reversed has absorbed too much of others' emotional reality and lost track of her own. Sometimes indicates someone projecting feelings onto others, or a person who uses emotional intelligence as a means of control.
