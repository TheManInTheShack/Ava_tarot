# Page of Cups

A young figure stands holding a cup, gazing into it with wonder and curiosity. A fish leaps from the cup—the unexpected, the magical, the creative surprise. This is the archetype of the sensitive, imaginative soul, the artist and dreamer.

## Upright

The Page of Cups is imagination, creativity, and openness to emotional and intuitive experience. This is the sensitive soul, the artist, the dreamer—someone who feels deeply and creates from that feeling. The Page brings messages of love, creative inspiration, or new emotional beginnings. In readings, the Page often represents a young person or a message about emotions or creativity. Or it may represent your own inner child—that part that creates freely, feels without judgment, and is open to wonder.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Page's openness becomes closed. The heart shutters; creativity dries up. Emotional immaturity or insensitivity; the Page creates destructively or refuses to create. Or escapism: using imagination to avoid reality rather than to enhance it. Sometimes reversed Page indicates that emotional sensitivity has been suppressed and needs to be recovered.
