# Three of Cups

Three figures stand together, raising their cups in celebration. They are joyful, united, surrounded by abundance—grapes hang overhead, the landscape is lush and fertile. This is the emotional counterpoint to the Wands' Three: not expansion outward but deepening of connection.

## Upright

The Three of Cups is celebration, friendship, and joyful community. A gathering is happening; people come together in mutual affection and shared joy. This can be literal—a party, a wedding, a reunion—or emotional and social. The card celebrates the happiness that comes from being known and accepted by others, from shared experience, from belonging. Where the Wands Three looks to the horizon, the Cups Three is present with loved ones. Often indicates positive social events, new friendships, or the strengthening of existing bonds.

## Reversed

Reversed, the gathering disperses or is troubled. Loneliness, isolation, or exclusion from community. The party is cancelled; the friends have scattered. Or overindulgence has become destructive, celebration turning to excess or chaos. Social withdrawal or inability to connect. Sometimes reversed suggests that isolation is needed—time alone to process emotions that have been suppressed in the name of social harmony.
