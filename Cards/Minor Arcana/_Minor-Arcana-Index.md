# Minor Arcana

56 cards across four suits. Each suit contains 14 cards: Ace through 10 (pip cards) and four court cards (Page, Knight, Queen, King).

## Suits and Elements

| Suit | Element | Domain | Prefix |
|------|---------|--------|--------|
| [[Wands/_Wands-Index\|Wands]] | Fire | Will, creativity, passion, career, ambition | WA |
| [[Cups/_Cups-Index\|Cups]] | Water | Emotion, relationships, intuition, the unconscious | CU |
| [[Swords/_Swords-Index\|Swords]] | Air | Mind, conflict, truth, communication, decision | SW |
| [[Pentacles/_Pentacles-Index\|Pentacles]] | Earth | Body, money, work, material world, nature | PE |

## Pip Number Meanings

Each suit runs through the same numerical arc:

| Number | Theme |
|--------|-------|
| **Ace (1)** | Pure seed of the element; undivided potential; gift from the divine |
| **2** | First choice; duality; balance or creative tension |
| **3** | Initial synthesis; collaboration; first results |
| **4** | Stability; foundation; rest; consolidation |
| **5** | Challenge; disruption; loss; the messy middle |
| **6** | Recovery; reciprocity; harmony; flowing forward |
| **7** | Assessment; strategy; inner work; the examined moment |
| **8** | Momentum; movement; mastery emerging |
| **9** | Near-completion; intensity; integration before the end |
| **10** | Culmination; the weight of completion; doorway to the next cycle |

## Court Card Roles

| Court | Developmental Stage | Elemental Modifier |
|-------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Page (11)** | Student; message-bearer; new energy entering | Earth of the suit's element |
| **Knight (12)** | Directed force in action; the quest | Air of the suit's element |
| **Queen (13)** | Mature embodiment; inward mastery; receptive authority | Water of the suit's element |
| **King (14)** | Outward authority; governance; the element fully commanded | Fire of the suit's element |
