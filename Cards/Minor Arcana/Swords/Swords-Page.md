# Page of Swords

A young figure stands holding a sword, alert and watchful, gaze fixed on the horizon. The posture is one of vigilance and curiosity—ready to cut through confusion to find truth.

## Upright

The Page of Swords is intellectual curiosity, the seeker of truth, the young mind that asks questions and wants to understand. This Page brings messages, news, communication, and the desire for clarity and information. In readings, the Page often represents a young person, a messenger, or a message. Or it represents your own inner Page—the part that wants to know, understand, and see clearly. The Page is watchful and alert, always scanning for threats or inconsistencies.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Page's clarity becomes confusion. The curiosity becomes prying or spying; truth-seeking becomes deception. Or naivety—the Page believes false information or does not have the discernment to separate truth from lie. Mental confusion, lack of clarity, the mind working against itself rather than for illumination.
