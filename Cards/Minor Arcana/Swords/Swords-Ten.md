# Ten of Swords

A figure lies face-down on the ground, ten swords plunged into their back. Yet the sky on the horizon is lightening — the darkest point of night giving way to dawn. A body of water beyond is calm. The Ten of Swords is unambiguous about devastation, but it contains — in the golden horizon — the seed of what follows.

## Upright

Rock bottom. The Ten of Swords marks the complete collapse of something — a situation, a relationship, a plan, an identity — through betrayal, crisis, or accumulated wounds. There is no softening this card. But the dawn in the background is real: rock bottom is also the place from which there is nowhere to go but up. The ending is absolute, and endings are also clearings.

## Reversed

The swords are being pulled out. Recovery begins, or — less hopefully — the querent is resisting an ending that has already happened, prolonging a situation that the universe has already concluded. Reversed Ten of Swords can also indicate the worst has been survived; the person is still here.
