# Six of Swords

A boatman poles a boat across calm water, carrying a figure away from the shore they are leaving. The journey is steady but purposeful; they are moving toward something new, away from what was. This is the card of passage and transition.

## Upright

The Six of Swords appears when you are moving from one place or condition to another. This may be literal travel, relocation, or a change of circumstance. Or it is metaphorical: moving past conflict, leaving behind what no longer serves, crossing from one phase of life to another. The journey is not necessarily easy, and you may still be carrying the weight of what you are leaving. But the direction is forward. The Six of Swords is about grace in transition and the wisdom that some things require movement away, not resolution in place.

## Reversed

Reversed, the transition is blocked. You want to move but cannot; circumstances trap you. Or you are unwilling to move even though staying costs you. Resistance to change, the desire to hold onto what must be released. The boat cannot sail; you are stuck on the shore.
