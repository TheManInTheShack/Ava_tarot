# Two of Swords

A figure is blindfolded, holding two swords that cross before them in a position of balance—precarious, tense, requiring constant attention. The figure cannot move without breaking the equilibrium. This is the card of stalemate, of conflict held in suspension.

## Upright

The Two of Swords appears when you are caught in an impasse—a decision cannot be made, a conflict cannot be resolved, both sides remain at sword's point. The blindfold indicates that clarity is obscured; you cannot see the way forward. This can be a necessary pause, a moment to gather information. But it can also become habitual—a refusal to choose, a preference for the devil you know rather than the unknown consequence of action. The Two of Swords asks: what decision are you avoiding? What blindfold are you wearing?

## Reversed

Reversed, the blindfold comes off; clarity returns. The stalemate breaks; one path becomes visible. A decision is made; the conflict is resolved or acknowledged as unresolvable and thus released. Communication becomes possible. This is movement after stagnation, even if the movement is simply accepting what is.
