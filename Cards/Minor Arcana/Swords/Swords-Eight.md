# Eight of Swords

A figure is bound and blindfolded, surrounded by eight swords standing upright in a circle. The figure appears trapped, powerless, unable to move. Yet looking closer, the bonds are loose; the escape is possible if the figure had courage to try.

## Upright

The Eight of Swords is restriction and the feeling of powerlessness. You are trapped by circumstances, by others' actions, or by your own beliefs about what is possible. The blindfold prevents you from seeing the way out; the swords form a cage. But the card carries a hidden message: the cage is not as solid as it appears. The binding is not as tight as you believe. The Eight of Swords asks: are you truly trapped, or have you accepted someone else's definition of your limits?

## Reversed

Reversed, the blindfold comes off; freedom becomes visible. You break the bonds; the way forward clears. Empowerment returns; you reclaim agency. Sometimes reversed indicates that the restriction was temporary and is now being released. Or it indicates that you have found the courage to break free despite the fear.
