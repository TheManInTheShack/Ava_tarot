# Suit of Swords

**Element**: Air  
**Domain**: Mind, thought, communication, conflict, truth, decision, intellect, consequence  
**Season**: Winter  
**Direction**: East  
**Court personalities**: Analytical, direct, decisive, sharp-tongued, principled, occasionally cold

The Swords suit traces the arc of mental experience and conflict: from the pure sword of absolute clarity (Ace) through decision, grief, betrayal, stalemate, intellectual mastery, and finally the King's impartial authority. Swords are often difficult cards — Air unchecked becomes the cruelty of pure logic without feeling.
