# Three of Swords

A heart pierced by three swords, floating against a stormy sky of rain and grey clouds. There is no figure in this card — just the wound, directly stated. The Three of Swords does not dress its meaning in narrative. Pain is pain. Grief is grief. The storm is real.

## Upright

Heartbreak, loss, or grief in clear and unavoidable form. The Three of Swords does not offer comfort directly; it offers honesty. Something has hurt — a rejection, a betrayal, a loss, a rupture in a relationship that mattered. The card's gift, if it has one, is clarity: this is what it is, and naming it is the first step through it. The swords in the heart are also the swords of Air — thought, word, truth. Sometimes the pain was caused by something said, or by a truth finally faced.

## Reversed

The swords are being removed, slowly. Recovery is underway, or grief is being processed and integrated. Reversed Three of Swords can also indicate someone who is refusing to let the healing happen — holding onto pain as an identity, refusing forgiveness (of self or other) because it feels like betrayal of the wound.
