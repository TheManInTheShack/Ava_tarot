# King of Swords

A king sits on his throne, holding a sword upright, his gaze commanding and clear. The background shows clouds and sky—the realm of thought and air. This King rules through intellect, logic, and the power of truth.

## Upright

The King of Swords is authority based on clarity and intellect. This King knows the truth and is not afraid to speak or enforce it. In readings, the King often represents a person (or the querent's own inner King) who is authoritative, logical, and commanding. Decisions made by the King are based on reason and clarity, not emotion. The King brings good news regarding intellectual matters, authority based on knowledge, power achieved through clear thinking. This is the CEO, the strategist, the one whose clarity commands respect.

## Reversed

Reversed, the King's intellect becomes tyranny. Truth becomes a weapon; logic becomes a way to control and dominate others. Cold, cruel intellectualism; the refusal to consider emotion or compassion. Or the King's authority is questioned; his logic is found wanting; his power is challenged. Sometimes reversed indicates that pure intellect without heart has become destructive.
