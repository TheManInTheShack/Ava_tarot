# Seven of Swords

A figure creeps away with five swords, leaving two behind. The figure's face is shadowed; their intent is unclear. This is the card of hidden action, deception, and the question of whether cunning is tactical wisdom or ethical betrayal.

## Upright

The Seven of Swords appears when deception is at play—either others are deceiving you, or you are engaging in it yourself. This can be thievery, cheating, or simply acting with hidden intentions. The card asks: what is being taken without permission? What is being hidden? The Seven is not necessarily evil—sometimes it represents necessary strategy, tactical thinking, or the wisdom of not revealing all your cards. But it carries warning: hidden actions have consequences when exposed.

## Reversed

Reversed, the deception is exposed. The truth comes to light; what was hidden is revealed. Humiliation follows for those who acted in shadow. Or innocence is proven; accusations are revealed as false. Sometimes reversed Seven indicates that attempted deception has failed and the person is forced to face consequences.
