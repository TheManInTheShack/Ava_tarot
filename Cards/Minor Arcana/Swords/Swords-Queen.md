# Queen of Swords

A queen sits on her throne, holding a sword aloft, her gaze clear and penetrating. She sees through illusion and pretense. Her bearing is regal and independent; she relies on her own intellect and perception rather than others' opinions.

## Upright

The Queen of Swords is intellectual clarity, sharp discernment, and the ability to see through confusion to truth. This Queen thinks independently and speaks her mind; she does not seek approval. In readings, the Queen often represents a person (or the querent's own inner Queen) who is intelligent, communicative, and perceptive. Or it may indicate a situation where clarity and honest assessment are needed. The Queen brings good news regarding intellectual matters, clear communication, and the ability to cut through confusion. She is not always comfortable—truth often isn't—but she is always clear.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Queen's clarity becomes rigid judgment. Intellect becomes cruelty; discernment becomes harsh criticism. The Queen becomes isolated, bitter, unable to soften logic with compassion. Or the clarity is lost; confusion prevails despite the Queen's attempts at control. Sometimes reversed indicates that isolation has become a problem and connection is needed to restore balance.
