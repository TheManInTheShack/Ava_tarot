# Knight of Swords

A figure on horseback charges forward with sword raised, wind streaming around both rider and horse. The expression is focused and determined; this Knight moves with intellectual force and clarity.

## Upright

The Knight of Swords is intellect in motion—sharp, clear, cutting. This Knight speaks truth directly, even when the truth is uncomfortable. Arguments are won through logic and clarity; obstacles are cut through rather than navigated around. In readings, the Knight often represents a person who is mentally acute, direct in communication, and unafraid to challenge falsehood. Or it represents your own inner Knight: the part that thinks clearly and speaks sharply. The Knight brings good news regarding intellectual pursuits, arguments won, clarity achieved.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Knight's sharp intellect becomes a weapon used carelessly or cruelly. Words wound; logic becomes twisted to serve a hidden agenda. Mental confusion, arguments that damage rather than clarify, careless speech that creates problems. Or the Knight is defeated by a stronger intellect; the clarity you counted on is not available.
