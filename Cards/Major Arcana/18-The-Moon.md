# The Moon

Two towers stand at the edge of a pool, and between them a path leads into the wilderness. A great moon hangs above, with a face both serene and unsettling. A dog and wolf howl toward the moon; a lobster emerges from the water. All things that are nocturnal, instinctive, ruled by the subconscious rather than reason. The Moon illuminates the hidden world, but its light casts strange shadows and makes solid ground uncertain.

## Upright

The Moon is the realm of dreams, intuition, and illusion. Here, reality is not what it appears to be; the conscious mind cannot navigate by reason alone. Fear arises in the Moon's shadows—not from external threat but from the unknown within yourself. Intuition is heightened; so is anxiety, confusion, and the sense that something is not as it seems. The Moon asks you to trust what you cannot see, to honor the subconscious realm as much as the rational. Often indicates a period of psychological work, dreams worth attending to, and the importance of distinguishing intuition from paranoia.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Moon begins to clear; illusion dissipates and truth becomes visible. What was hidden comes to light. The confusion lifts, fear loses its grip. However, reversed can also mean refusing the subconscious—denying intuition, rejecting the dreamworld, insisting on logic alone. Balance is restored when both mind and intuition are honored.

## Elemental and Astrological

Pisces/Neptune: dissolution of boundaries, the mystical, the realm where all things merge. Neptune rules both divine inspiration and delusion; the Moon shows both as faces of the same mystery. Pisces is the dreamer, the mystic, the one who dissolves into the infinite.
