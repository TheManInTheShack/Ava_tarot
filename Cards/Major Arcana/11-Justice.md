# Justice

A crowned figure sits between two pillars — echoing the High Priestess — holding a sword upright in one hand and scales balanced in the other. The veil behind them is partly open. Justice is not blind here; she sees clearly and deliberately. The sword cuts to truth; the scales weigh it.

## Upright

The law of cause and effect made visible. Decisions have consequences; actions have weight. This card often appears in readings about legal matters, fairness disputes, or karmic reckoning — situations where what was done in the past is now being measured. It also appears as a reminder that honesty and accountability are not punishments but foundations. If you have acted with integrity, Justice is your ally.

## Reversed

Truth obscured or scales tipped. Someone is being judged unfairly, or the querent is avoiding responsibility for their own actions. Can indicate actual legal trouble, or an internal refusal to reckon honestly with consequences. Self-deception is the reversed Justice reading no one wants to hear.

## Elemental and Astrological

Air/Libra: the cardinal air sign of balance and relationship. Venus rulership might seem soft for a card of accountability, but Justice is ultimately about relational truth — how we weigh ourselves against our actions and against others.
