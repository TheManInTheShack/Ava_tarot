# Strength

A figure in white gently closes the mouth of a lion — not by force, but by presence. The lion doesn't fight. The lemniscate (infinity symbol) hovers above the figure's head, the same one worn by the Magician. Strength is not the absence of fear or the dominance of brute force; it is the quiet mastery that comes from knowing one's own nature well enough to meet the beast without flinching.

## Upright

Inner courage expressed through gentleness. This card appears when the situation calls not for aggression but for patient, compassionate persistence — calming a difficult person, managing one's own volatile emotions, enduring a long trial without breaking. The lion is not an enemy; it is the wild self. Strength is the capacity to love that part rather than suppress it.

## Reversed

The lion wins. Self-doubt undermines action; raw emotion overtakes reason; fear is mistaken for wisdom. Reversed Strength can also indicate the opposite problem — brute force applied where patience was needed, cruelty dressed up as toughness.

## Elemental and Astrological

Fire and Leo: the sun at full noon, radiant and controlled. The Strength card is not about subduing fire — it's about becoming so comfortable with it that it follows you willingly.
