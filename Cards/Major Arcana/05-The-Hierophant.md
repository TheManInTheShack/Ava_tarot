# The Hierophant

The Hierophant — also called the Pope in older decks — sits between two pillars as the High Priestess does, but where she guards mysteries, he transmits them. He raises one hand in blessing and holds a triple cross scepter in the other. Two acolytes kneel before him. He is the bridge between the divine and the institutional: the keeper of tradition, the teacher of doctrine.

## Upright

Tradition, established institutions, and the wisdom held in collective knowledge. The Hierophant invites you to seek guidance from established sources — a mentor, a tradition, a community, a body of practice. There is wisdom in what has been passed down. He can also indicate marriage, formal agreements, or entry into an institution.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Hierophant challenges the querent to question received wisdom. Not all tradition is wisdom — some is just calcification. He reversed can indicate breaking from a group, forging a personal spiritual path, or exposing the gap between an institution's stated values and its actual behavior.

## Elemental and Astrological

Taurus-ruled like the Empress, but where she embodies Venus as beauty and sensory abundance, the Hierophant embodies Venus as the desire for order, harmony, and preservation. Earth grounds his teachings in form, structure, and continuity.
