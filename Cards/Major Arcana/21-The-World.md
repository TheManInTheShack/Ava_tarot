# The World

A figure dances within a wreath of laurel and oak, surrounded by the four fixed signs of the zodiac (eagle, lion, ox, angel). This is the completion of the Fool's Journey, the wholeness that comes from integrating all that has been learned. The World is not an ending but a new beginning from a place of integration and fulfillment.

## Upright

The World is the card of completion and fulfillment. A cycle has ended; a goal has been achieved; a journey is complete. This is not a resting place but a threshold—you have integrated what the journey taught, and now you are whole enough to begin again, at a higher level of understanding. The World brings a sense of wholeness, of standing at the center of your life with all the pieces integrated. Often appears at the end of a project, period, or relationship—not as loss but as graduation. It is also a card of travel, of the world becoming accessible, of expansion into new horizons.

## Reversed

Reversed, the World suggests the cycle has not yet closed, the integration is incomplete. Unfinished business, the sense that something is still needed to bring closure. You may be holding on to something that is ready to be released, or refusing to move forward into the new cycle. Fragmentation instead of wholeness; the pieces of your life not yet integrated. The remedy is to complete what remains and allow the old cycle to close so the new can begin.

## Elemental and Astrological

Saturn/Earth: the manifestation of all that was potential in the Fool's leap. Saturn here is not limitation but the solidity and permanence that comes from completing a full cycle. Earth grounds; the World is the earthly manifestation of spiritual completion.
