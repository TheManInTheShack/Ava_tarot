# Major Arcana

The 22 trump cards of the tarot, numbered 0–21. Together they trace the **Fool's Journey** — a mythic arc of individuation from innocent potential (The Fool) through every domain of human experience to integrated wholeness (The World).

## The Fool's Journey

```
[00] The Fool           Innocent beginning; the leap before the map
[01] The Magician       Focused will; the tools are all present
[02] The High Priestess Inner knowing; the veil between worlds
[03] The Empress        Fertile creation; abundance; the body
[04] The Emperor        Structure, authority, and the outer world
[05] The Hierophant     Tradition, teaching, and the institutions of belief
[06] The Lovers         Values, choice, and conscious union
[07] The Chariot        Discipline, drive, and the will to move
[08] Strength           Courage, patience, and the taming of instinct
[09] The Hermit         Solitude, inner light, and the wisdom of withdrawal
[10] Wheel of Fortune   Cycles, turning points, and fate
[11] Justice            Truth, balance, and consequence
[12] The Hanged Man     Surrender, new perspective, and sacred pause
[13] Death              Transformation, endings, and what cannot be taken
[14] Temperance         Integration, moderation, and alchemy in motion
[15] The Devil          Shadow, binding, and the chains we do not see
[16] The Tower          Disruption, revelation, and the collapse of the false
[17] The Star           Hope, renewal, and the sky after the storm
[18] The Moon           Dreams, illusion, and the unconscious unguarded
[19] The Sun            Joy, clarity, and unobstructed life force
[20] Judgement          Awakening, reckoning, and the call to become
[21] The World          Completion, integration, and the moment before the next Fool
```
