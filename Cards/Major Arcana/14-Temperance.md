# Temperance

An angel — one foot in water, one on land — pours liquid between two cups in an eternal flow. The triangle within the square on their chest represents the harmony of spirit within the material world. Irises grow by the water's edge; a golden path leads to the mountains and a rising sun. Temperance is alchemy: the patient transformation of elements into something greater than their sum.

## Upright

The art of blending. This card appears when patience and moderation are the path forward — when the answer is not more of any one thing but the right proportion of many things. Life circumstances that have felt chaotic after Death's clearing are now ready to be integrated. Temperance asks: can you hold two things at once without forcing either? Can you let the process take the time it needs?

## Reversed

The mixing has gone wrong — too much of one element, too little of another. Excess in some domain (work, pleasure, emotion, discipline) that disrupts the whole. Or a situation that demands patience but is receiving urgency. Realignment is needed; the flow has been forced or broken.

## Elemental and Astrological

Sagittarius/Jupiter: the fire of aspiration and the expansion of wisdom. Where earlier fire cards (Chariot, Strength) were about mastery and will, Temperance's fire is the flame of ongoing purpose — the long arc of becoming.
