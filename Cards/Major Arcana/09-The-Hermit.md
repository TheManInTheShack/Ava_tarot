# The Hermit

An elder stands alone on a mountain peak, cloaked and still, holding a lantern aloft. The light is not for the crowd; it illuminates only the next step. The Hermit has climbed away from the world not in defeat but in purpose — to find what cannot be found in noise.

## Upright

Voluntary solitude in service of inner work. A time to go inward, to stop seeking answers outside and begin listening for the ones already present. This card often appears when the querent is being called to step back from external demands — to reflect, to grieve quietly, to integrate what has been learned. The lantern is wisdom: the Hermit doesn't claim to see the whole path, only the next step.

## Reversed

Solitude that has curdled into isolation. The retreat has gone on too long, or was never chosen freely to begin with. Reversed Hermit can indicate refusing guidance, cutting oneself off from community out of fear rather than need, or being so absorbed in self-examination that action never comes.

## Elemental and Astrological

Earth/Virgo: the mountain is solid, the climb is methodical. Virgo's Mercury rulership speaks to the Hermit's analytical inner work — the mind turned toward discernment, sorting wheat from chaff in the psyche.
