# The Sun

A bright sun shines in a clear sky above a landscape of simple joy. A child rides a horse, free and unburdened. Sunflowers turn toward the light. There is no shadow, no hidden meaning—the Sun is radiance itself. This is the card of total clarity, absolute success, the world as it truly is without illusion or fear.

## Upright

The Sun is one of the most fortunate cards in the deck. It represents success, achievement, joy, and a period of clarity and confidence. This is when the work pays off, when obstacles clear, when the path forward is illuminated. The Sun brings optimism, vitality, the sense of being alive and aligned with your purpose. It is not complicated; it is direct and warm. Often appears during periods of healing, as the darkness passes and life returns to fullness. The Sun is also literal—it can indicate travel, time in nature, a positive outcome to a situation that seemed unclear.

## Reversed

Reversed, the Sun's light is temporarily obscured—not by external clouds but by temporary obscuring of your own vision. Burnout, arrogance that precedes a fall, success that has become excessive and unbalanced. The Sun reversed can also indicate that you are standing in someone else's shadow, or that your own light is being dimmed by exhaustion. The remedy is to remember that the Sun always shines; your task is to ensure you are facing it.

## Elemental and Astrological

Sun: the ultimate source of all Fire and warmth. Pure elemental expression of vitality, consciousness, and the divine spark. The Sun is the center around which all orbits; in the tarot, it represents your own core truth and the light of consciousness itself.
